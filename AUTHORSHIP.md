## How the work was done

The project ran in stages. Each stage ended with a checkpoint: open questions about the stage's proofs, answered by
the author without notes and checked before the next stage began. Several answers went beyond the questions, and those
results are now in the paper:

| In the paper | What the author contributed |
|---|---|
| Theorem 3.3 | The one-range threshold extends to any noise law satisfying condition (C) |
| Proposition 3.5 | The growth law for Laplace noise, κ<sub>d</sub> = d/2 + (z/2)√d + (z² − 1)/6 + O(d<sup>−1/2</sup>) |
| Lemma 4.10 | Critical points on the anchors' subspace are transverse maxima, with the author's proof |
| Section 4.5 | The false flip ambiguity, anchor emission, the weighted Fermat point, and four anchors with two maxima |
| Remark 4.16 | Two ranges under other noise laws |

The dated checkpoint records are available on request.
